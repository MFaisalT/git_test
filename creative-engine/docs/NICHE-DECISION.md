## 2. Unit economics (transparent assumptions; all numbers are owner-editable inputs, not forecasts)

Fixed assumptions: existing ChatGPT + Higgsfield subscriptions are sunk cash; credits have opportunity cost (981.16 credits on 2026-10-07; quotes 8 credits/8 s mini, 56 credits/8 s Seedance 2.5; quotes ≠ measured cost; retakes 2–4× per shot is an untested sensitivity). Owner time valued at an owner-chosen rate `r` per hour (left symbolic). Ads: $0 in pilot (the <$50/month preference is context, not approval).

### N1 Character page (16-post organic pilot per method-and-pilot.md)
- Production cost: 32–128 generations → 256–1,024 credits (mini) or 1,792–7,168 (Seedance 2.5) + stills/voice. Owner time 25–55 h provisional.
- Qualified reach: unknown until measured; data-adequacy floor is ≥5,000 unique accounts on 3 of 4 follow-up posts, ≥20 shares and ≥20 attributed follows per concept batch (a convention, not a success threshold).
- Revenue scenarios at month 3: **zero** (most likely given zero verified cold-start cases); **downside**: credits exhausted before signal, owner time 40 h, no deal; **upside hypothesis**: one sponsorship inquiry (fee unknown; no rate card evidence exists in the research).
- Conversion assumptions: none defensible yet. Followers ≠ profit.

### N2 Commercial creator niche
- Production cost: lower per post (talking head/b-roll), 10–20 posts/month at ~1–2 generations each → 80–320 credits/month on mini.
- Monetization: affiliate (assume 1–3% click-to-purchase on 1–5% click-through of reach; commissions 3–10%) → at 50K monthly reach that is roughly tens of dollars; sponsorships need 10K+ engaged followers and a jurisdiction-compliant disclosure path.
- Scenarios: zero (likely for 2–3 months); downside: content accurate-but-ignored; upside: a niche affiliate programme covering subscription cost.

### N3 Productized service
- Offer: brief → planning-ready packet (+ optional render) for a small brand. Price hypothesis $150–$600 per spot (unverified; market check required before quoting). Gross margin high on planning (owner time ~2–4 h per packet with the engine); render adds credits (≈8–56 credits per 8 s shot + retakes) and QA time.
- Acquisition: 20 outreach messages → 1–2 replies → 0–1 paid pilot (assumption; measure).
- Scenarios: zero (no sale in 60 days); downside: unpaid revisions eat margin; upside: 2 clients/month ≈ $300–$1,200 gross before owner time.
- Time to first revenue: 2–6 weeks if outreach starts; requires contracting, disclosure and rights handling the owner has not yet authorised (contact with others requires approval).

## 3. Platform rules that gate any native revenue (official pages; captured by the lab 2026-10-07, re-checked today where reachable)

Official domains tiktok.com / support.google.com / help.instagram.com are **egress-blocked** from this container; the lab's same-day captures in `commercial-report.md` are the basis and remain dated:
- TikTok Creator Rewards: 18+, personal account in good standing, 10K followers, 100K views/30 days, eligible country, original videos longer than one minute → a sub-minute comedy pilot does **not** qualify; Duets/Stitches/sponsored excluded.
- YouTube Partner Program: 1,000 subs + 4,000 public watch hours/12 months or 10M Shorts views/90 days; from **1 Feb 2027** new entrants need 8,000 hours or 20M Shorts views; "inauthentic/repetitious content" policy applies to mass-produced AI output; AI-altered realistic content must be disclosed.
- Instagram: no universal per-view payout established; Gifts/subscriptions/bonuses are invite- and region-gated (historic 5,000-follower Gifts threshold; current requirement unverified). Meta "AI info" label applies via signals or self-disclosure; a May 2026 report describes an "AI Creator" profile label (secondary source; unverified).
- X: Original Content Rewards requires Premium, 500 verified followers, 500K verified impressions/90 days, and excludes content "created or posted using automated means" → unresolved eligibility for an AI-character workflow.
- Disclosure: FTC (US) material-connection disclosure and no claims of unexperienced product use; EU AI Act Article 50 transparency from 2 Aug 2026; UAE/KSA advertiser permits if the owner is there. Jurisdiction unknown → unresolved.

## 4. Recommendation

**Primary path: N1 as the learning engine, with N3 as the monetisation wrapper.** Build the original character page as a bounded 16-post organic experiment (it is what the lab was set up for and what the engine is tuned to), but treat the engine's brief→packet capability as the product that can earn first: sell planning-ready character-driven spots to small brands once the owner authorises outreach. This hedges the main risk (cold-start virality is unproven) with a route that does not require followers.

**Fallback: N2.** If after the 16-post pilot the character has no repeatable share/follow signal and no service inquiry has converted, pivot the same character into a utility format (organisation/household tips with the Inspector framing), which trades novelty for steadier search-driven demand.

**Original character: develop it** (it improves N1 and N3; a recognisable recurring character is the asset a brand pays for). Hypothesis `inspector-v1` is used here because it ranked first in comedy-report.md and its self-own rule gives payoffs a structural engine; it is not a validated winner.

## 5. What evidence would change the decision
- A dated, creator-owned follower history proving a strict organic cold start (would raise N1 priority).
- Two paid service inquiries before the pilot ends (would make N3 primary outright).
- Owner jurisdiction with confirmed TikTok/YouTube eligibility (would make longer-form formats worth the cost).
- Pilot medians below the data-adequacy floor on 3 of 4 follow-ups per concept (would trigger the N2 fallback or stop).
